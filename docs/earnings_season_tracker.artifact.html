What the numbers say

  • Breadth above average, magnitude below

    82% of reporters beat, 4 pts above the 5-yr average (78%). In aggregate they beat by +1.9% against a +7.5% norm (+8.0% over 10 years). Sector extremes: Health Care +13.2%, Communication Services -21.5%.

  • Revenue beats are running above average

    Revenue beats run at 70% vs 70% (5-yr) and 68% (10-yr), with a +1.7% aggregate surprise vs +1.8%.

  • Guidance leans positive

    28 of 47 companies guiding Q4 2026 came in above consensus (60% vs ≈40% norm). The pre-season Q3 2026 read: 72 of 116 (62%). Annual guidance is 66% positive.

  • Growth is re-accelerating

    Blended Q3 2026 growth is 30.2% and TTM growth 31.3%, the highest since Q1'22. Q2'26: Includes GAAP investment gains at Alphabet ($98bn) and Amazon ($53.4bn); 33.8% excluding the two.

  • Q4 is the seasonal weak spot

    Since 2022, Q4 seasons have printed 68%, 73%, 75%, 73%, below the 5-yr average in 4 of 4 years and averaging 72% against 79% for other quarters.

  • Treat 60% reported as provisional

    Recent seasons moved 1 to 4 pts between a quarter-way read and the close (table at left). The current print sits 4 pts over the average, within the range drift has covered.

Definitions

  • Beat: actual EPS above the mean estimate (FactSet). In-line and misses are split out above.
  • Positive guidance: a company's EPS guidance (or range midpoint) is above the mean estimate the day before it was issued. FactSet does not publish a "% raising guidance" series; this is its closest measure. Guidance is either positive or negative, so the ≈40% norm is 100% minus FactSet's 60% negative average.
  • Reported count (≈300) is derived: 60% of FactSet's 500-company base.

Data notes

  • Every completed quarter is a late-season reading (94% or more of companies reported). FactSet's later citations can differ slightly: Q3'23 beat rate 81% later vs 82% at 98%; Q2'21 growth 91.6% vs 90.9%; Q4'21 growth 32.0% vs 30.7% at 95%.
  • TTM growth: FactSet's quarterly y/y rates for the last four quarters, weighted by the year-ago quarters' bottom-up EPS. A plain ratio of summed bottom-up EPS runs within 0.9 pts on average (max 3.5), since FactSet's rates compare the same companies year on year.
  • Guidance counts are pre-season, from the edition nearest quarter end. Q3'22: FactSet gave no 5-yr percentage that quarter, so the average line is bridged.
  • 5-yr averages are as quoted in-season by FactSet, not recomputed.
